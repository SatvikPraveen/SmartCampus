# ITC-2007 post-enrolment benchmark summary

Instances: 24. Seeds per instance: 10. Local-search iterations: 1000000. Intervals are 95% percentile-bootstrap CIs of the mean (10000 resamples).

*Feasible* means every event placed with no hard violation (the engine's definition). *DtF* and *ITC soft* are the competition's measures after unplacing events until no hard violation remains (`ItcScore`).
